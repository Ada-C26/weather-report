## Accessibility Needs
*What does each team member need access to in order to succeed and show up the best they can?*
Access to clear communication, project requirements, shared resources, tools, and timely feedback.

## Collaboration vs. Individual Work Expectations
*Clarify your collaboration expectations - does your group want to write code together all of the time? Or divide work to do independently, then come together to share accomplishments? What tools and technologies can help your collaboration?*
We prefer to divide tasks and work independently when appropriate, then come together to share progress, review code, and solve problems. We can also pair program when needed. Tools like GitHub, Slack, and Zoom can help us communicate and collaborate.

## Learning Style
*How does each team member learn best in project settings?*
We learn best through hands-on practice, step-by-step explanations, asking questions, sharing ideas, and learning from each other's feedback. Pair programming and reviewing code together are also helpful.

## Preferred Feedback Style
*How does each team member best receive feedback?*
We prefer feedback that is respectful, constructive, specific, and timely, with clear suggestions for improvement and open communication.

## One Team Communication Skill to Improve
*What is a teamwork-related skill you want to work on?*
I want to improve my communication skills by sharing ideas more confidently, asking questions, and actively listening to my teammates.
